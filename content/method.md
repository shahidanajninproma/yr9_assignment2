**Scenario model.** Capacity(year) = 2025 base (CBS) + dated RVO sites in their expected year + delay (sites pushed past 2032 drop out) + Gamma-A/B only if the tender succeeds + undated GW spread evenly over 2030-2032. Delay applies to dated sites only. Sliders and presets are **illustrative assumptions**, not data.

**Forecast.** Ordinary least squares on offshore capacity 2015-2025 with a ~95% prediction interval (Student t). **Validation.** Train to the holdout year, test the later years, compare mean absolute error (MAE) with a naive last-value benchmark. With only a few test points, results are indicative.

**Colour code.** Blue = observed data. Orange dashed = model forecast. Green = scenario assumptions.

**Known limits.** 2024-2025 CBS values are marked provisional; RVO dates are expectations ("about" GW); the 2026-2027 range for Hollandse Kust (west) VI is read as 2027; the 21 GW target comes from an older RVO planning note, while the May 2026 Roadmap states 23 GW (change the target in the sidebar).
