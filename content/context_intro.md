**Why the Netherlands, why offshore wind.** The Dutch North Sea is a leading European offshore wind location, and policy sets a 2032 capacity target. For a **supplier** of installation, cables or vessels, the question is whether to invest in Dutch capacity to 2032. PESTLE explains the *environment*; Porter's Diamond explains the *competitiveness*. Each row below is tied to the dashboard indicator it explains.

**Time horizon: 2032.** This is the official target year and about the time suppliers need to commit assets (vessels and cable lines take years to build).
