### No-regret moves (useful in all three scenarios)
- Secure positions on **dated** sites (Hollandse Kust (west), IJmuiden Ver Alpha and Beta); they supply most volume to 2032 in every scenario.
- Keep capacity **flexible** (charter, lease, partner) rather than dedicated.
- Build export and service options so Dutch timing risk is not fatal.

### Contingent moves (depend on one future)
- **Roadmap delivered:** commit extra vessel and cable capacity for the 2030-2032 peak.
- **Delayed build-out:** re-phase investment; hold back until contracts and grid dates firm up.
- **Cost squeeze:** avoid Dutch-specific assets; wait for two-way contracts (CfD).

### Signals to watch
- Gamma tender result (Q4 2026 permit procedure per the Roadmap).
- First CfD offshore round (planned towards end-2027).
- Whether tenders for Nederwiek and Doordewind sites receive bids and get dates.
- Grid-connection and platform delivery dates.
- Next update of the Offshore Wind Energy Roadmap.

*Scenarios are possible futures, not predictions, and carry no probabilities.*
