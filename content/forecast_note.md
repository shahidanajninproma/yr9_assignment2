**Why a trend is only a benchmark.** Offshore capacity arrives in **steps** (whole farms of 0.7-2 GW commission at once), so a straight line cannot predict timing. It answers: *what if the 2015-2025 pace simply continued?* The shaded band is an approximate 95% prediction interval from the fit; it widens with horizon but cannot capture step changes. The pipeline scenarios on the Scenarios tab are the decision tool; the trend is the yardstick.
